## B. Fully detailed image-generation prompt

> ChatGPT (gpt-image), Google Gemini "Nano-Banana", or Ideogram. Midjourney makes it pretty
> but garbles long text. For a *real* editable file keep the DOCX; use this prompt for a quick
> visual mock-up. Generate at the page's aspect ratio (landscape **1.41:1**, or portrait **1:1.41**).
